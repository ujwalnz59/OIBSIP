# Exploratory Data Analysis on Retail Sales Data

## Oasis Infobyte Data Analytics Internship

### Task
Level 1 - Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on Retail Sales Data

## Project Overview
This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on the Sample Superstore dataset. The objective is to analyze sales performance, profitability, product performance, seasonal trends, and the relationship between discounts and profit.

## Tools and Technologies
- Python
- Google Colab
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn

## Analysis Performed
- Dataset understanding and inspection
- Missing value analysis
- Duplicate value analysis
- Total sales by category
- Top 10 best-selling products
- Monthly sales trend
- Quarterly sales trend
- Correlation heatmap
- Total profit by category
- Discount vs Profit analysis

## Key Findings
- Technology generated the highest total sales and profit.
- Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier was the highest-selling product.
- Sales showed strong performance during the final quarter of the year.
- Furniture generated substantial sales but comparatively low profit.
- Higher discounts were associated with lower profitability.

## Conclusion
The Exploratory Data Analysis provided useful insights into the sales and profitability of the Superstore dataset. Technology was the strongest-performing category, while Furniture showed comparatively low profitability despite substantial sales. The analysis also showed that higher discounts were associated with lower profits. These findings can help support better inventory planning, pricing strategies, promotional decisions, and overall business decision-making.
