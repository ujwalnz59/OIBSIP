# Customer Segmentation Analysis

## Oasis Infobyte Data Analytics Internship

### Task
Level 1 - Customer Segmentation Analysis

## Project Overview
This project focuses on customer segmentation using the Sample Superstore dataset. The objective is to analyze customer purchasing behavior and divide customers into different groups based on their total sales.

## Tools and Technologies
- Python
- Google Colab
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn

## Analysis Performed
- Dataset loading and inspection
- Customer-level data aggregation
- Total sales analysis
- Total quantity analysis
- Total profit analysis
- Order frequency analysis
- Top 10 customers by sales
- Customer segmentation
- Sales vs Profit analysis
- Sales contribution by customer segment
- Profit contribution by customer segment

## Customer Segments
Customers were divided into three groups:

- Low Value Customers
- Medium Value Customers
- High Value Customers

## Key Insights
High-value customers contribute significantly to overall sales and are important for business growth and customer retention. Medium-value customers have the potential to become high-value customers through targeted offers and loyalty programs. Low-value customers can be encouraged to purchase more frequently through suitable promotions.

## Recommendations
- Provide loyalty rewards to high-value customers.
- Target medium-value customers with personalized promotions.
- Encourage low-value customers to purchase more frequently.
- Monitor customers with high sales but low profitability.
- Avoid excessive discounts that may reduce profitability.

## Conclusion
The customer segmentation analysis successfully divided customers into Low Value, Medium Value, and High Value groups based on their total sales. The analysis helps businesses understand customer purchasing behavior and identify their most valuable customers. Customer segmentation can support better marketing strategies, customer retention, personalized promotions, and business decision-making.
